Perfect Shapes — Privacy Policy
Pre-release draft · Updated October 19, 2026

This draft describes the current build and the planned use of Google AdMob. The publisher is Ramedusa Studios. The intended audience and the final advertising configuration must be confirmed before this policy or the game is published.

1. Information stored on your device

Perfect Shapes stores game progress, completed levels, placed pieces and rotations, virtual coin balances, Special unlocks, reward-claim records, tutorial status, a completed-puzzle counter for interstitial ad breaks, and music, sound and vibration settings on your device. Previous save copies may be kept to recover interrupted or damaged saves.

The current game does not require an account. Its game code does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server or provide cloud synchronization. Your device's own backup and restore features may also apply.

2. Internet connection checks

An internet connection is required to play. The game makes small HTTPS requests to Google's connectivitycheck.gstatic.com and Microsoft's www.msftconnecttest.com to check whether the connection can reach the internet. A Wi-Fi sign-in page does not count as a successful check.

These requests disclose your IP address and ordinary connection information to the service receiving them. We do not attach your saved progress, virtual coins, reward history, email address or an advertising identifier to these checks. The service providers handle their own connection logs under their privacy policies.

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Microsoft: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

3. Advertising

Google AdMob is the selected advertising service. The current pre-release Android debug build integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's rewarded and interstitial demo ad units for testing. Production ad requests remain disabled in release builds. Desktop builds do not request native advertisements. Test advertisements are not associated with the publisher's advertising unit; SDK network requests can still involve the data described below.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, interactions with the app or advertisements, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The IP address may be used to estimate general location. The exact practices must be checked against the SDK version, configuration and any additional advertising partners actually included in the released app.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google Mobile Ads data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Before advertising is enabled or a monetized version is published, this policy and the store disclosures must be finalized for the actual integration, including any applicable consent, privacy options and age-related settings. No specific consent or advertising-choice mechanism is claimed to be implemented in this build. An internet connection alone does not mean an advertisement is available.

4. How information is used

On-device records are used to resume puzzles, remember preferences, maintain virtual rewards, unlock content and avoid granting the same advertisement reward more than once. Connectivity checks are used to enforce the game's internet requirement.

The current game code does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location. This statement must be reviewed again if future features or third-party SDKs are added.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Local saves remain in the app's storage until they are reset, cleared or removed. Clearing the app's storage removes its local progress, coins, unlocks, settings and backup files. Device backup/restore settings may affect what is restored after a reinstall.

The game's local data is stored in the operating system's app storage. Save checksums and recovery copies help detect incomplete writes and recover earlier records; they are not encryption or a guarantee against every device failure. Network checks use HTTPS.

There is no developer account or developer-operated cloud-save database in the current build. Retention of connection logs held by the connectivity services is governed by those services.

6. Children and intended audience

The intended age group has not yet been finalized. The publisher must confirm it before release and configure any advertising, consent and age-related protections accordingly. This draft must not be treated as a completed child-directed-app disclosure.

7. Changes

This policy must be updated when the game's data practices, advertising provider, audience or online features change. The published policy will show its effective or update date.
