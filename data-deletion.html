Perfect Shapes — Data Deletion

Developer: Ramedusa Studios
Last updated: October 29, 2026

You can request deletion of information you have provided to Ramedusa Studios. Perfect Shapes does not require an account and does not provide cloud saves.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email ramedusastudios.support@gmail.com with the subject Perfect Shapes - Data Deletion Request.
  2. Describe the information you want deleted, such as a previous support conversation or an attachment. If possible, send the request from the email address used in that conversation.
  3. We review your request, ask only for information necessary to identify the relevant records, and reply with the result or any applicable limitation. You do not need to install the game or create an account to send a request.

Please do not send passwords, identity documents, your home address, or other unnecessary sensitive information. A parent or guardian may contact us about information they have provided concerning their child.

Information we can delete

Where identifiable and held by Ramedusa Studios, this includes support emails, the email address and other personal information in those conversations, and attachments you sent us. We do not operate a server-side database of your puzzle progress, coins, Special unlocks or entered age.

Delete game data stored on your device

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Perfect Shapes → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Names may differ by device. This removes local puzzle progress, coins, unlocks, age category, preferences and recovery copies. It resets your local game and cannot be undone by us. Operating-system backup or device-transfer features may affect whether old data is restored.

Retention and exceptions

Local game records remain on your device until you clear them. Support correspondence is retained while needed to respond and follow up, or where a legal obligation requires retention. There is no automatic 90-day deletion promise.

No standard additional retention period is applied to information we delete in response to a request. If a legal requirement or an ongoing matter requires specific records to be retained, we explain the reason and applicable retention period in our response. We handle requests within the applicable legal time limits.

Information handled by other providers

Google advertising or diagnostic services, connectivity providers, and email providers may hold technical information under their own policies. Clearing local storage or deleting a support email does not automatically erase their independently held records. We do not promise to erase records we cannot access or identify. We can explain the relevant provider and available request route when responding.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Microsoft's Privacy Statement, and the Perfect Shapes Privacy Policy.
