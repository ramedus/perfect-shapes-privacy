Account &
data deletion

Updated November 22, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Perfect Shapes account and associated data. A parent or guardian may contact us on a child's behalf. Updated builds let both guests and Google-connected players submit a request from Settings → Request account deletion → Send request. Where the server processor is enabled, queued requests are processed automatically. Sending a request does not mean deletion has already completed.

Without access to the game

Email requests are verified and handled manually by Ramedusa Studios.

  1. Email ramedusastudios.support@gmail.com with the subject Perfect Shapes account/data deletion.
  2. Include your Player ID, copied from Settings, if available. If Google is linked, sending from that address can help verify the request.
  3. Tell us whether you want the entire account deleted or a particular category of information removed. We may ask for proportionate information to locate the record and verify the request.

Do not send passwords, card details, refresh tokens, raw purchase tokens or service keys. If you have an anonymous account, contact us before clearing app storage so you can still copy its Player ID.

What happens to the data?

After verifying the request, we delete or de-identify eligible account and linked game records, such as supported progress, preferences, calendar associations and reward records. Account deletion can remove access to saved progress, balances and purchased game benefits. Tell us if a paid purchase or pending delivery needs review before deletion.

Necessary financial, security, anti-fraud or legal records may be retained where required or justified. Backups and provider logs may follow separate retention cycles. There is no blanket expiry of inactive accounts. The account deletion processor retains minimal purchase-token and Play account SHA-256 hashes to prevent duplicate grants, plus deletion timestamps and a request receipt; these hashes are not guaranteed anonymous and currently have no automatic expiry. It removes the account UUID from the completed request. Raw/encrypted purchase tokens, order IDs and save files are not kept in those retained anti-fraud records. After a deletion request is successfully submitted, updated builds clear that account's local profile and start a separate fresh guest game. Server deletion continues in the background; offline devices may retain copies until reconnecting and checking deletion status. Provider-held logs, payment records and support emails require separate handling where applicable.

Uninstalling is different

Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local game records and credentials. It does not automatically erase server information. Removing Google's connection permission does not delete the game account, and deleting the game account does not delete your Google account. Google Play purchase refunds are handled separately.

Questions or missing Player ID?

Email us with the information you still have. We will explain what is needed to locate the relevant record. We cannot promise to identify an anonymous account from an unrelated email address alone.
