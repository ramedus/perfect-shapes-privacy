Google is optional.

Updated versions create an anonymous game account automatically. You can choose to connect with Google in Settings to link your identity and help with account recovery.

Google connection uses basic profile information for sign-in. It does not give the game access to your Gmail, contacts or Drive.

You can play and purchase without connecting Google. Complete cross-device progress synchronization is not currently available.

Before you play

A few helpful answers.

Is Perfect Shapes free to play?

The Android game is free to download and contains ads and optional in-app purchases. Offers and prices shown by Google Play can vary by country and version.

Do I need to connect a Google account?

No. Google connection is optional. In updated builds an anonymous account supports game records and purchases without requiring an email address.

Where can I ask about my account or data?

Email ramedusastudios.support@gmail.com. For a deletion request, follow the account and data deletion instructions. Keep your Player ID from Settings if available.
